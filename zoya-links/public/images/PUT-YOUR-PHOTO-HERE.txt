Add your portrait to this folder as:  zoya.jpg

Tips
- Portrait orientation, about 4:5 (for example 1200 x 1500 px)
- Keep your face in the upper half; the bottom third fades into black behind your name and icons
- Under about 400 KB (export as JPG, quality ~80)
- To use another file name, change "photo" in public/config.json and the src in public/index.html
